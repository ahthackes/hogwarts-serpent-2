The Rules

Eat the glowing treasures to grow. Every level has a target — reach it to pass. Three stars are awarded for finishing, for beating the par time, and for a high score.

Easy mode: sail through walls and reappear on the opposite side of the board, just like the classic mobile snake. Difficult mode: every wall, your own tail and the guardians are fatal. Hell mode: endless only, far faster, far deadlier — every wall kills and the guardians arrive almost immediately.

Guardians

Every sixth level is watched by a guardian. Their attacks are telegraphed on the floor — move out of the marked ground before it strikes.

Coins & Skins

Survive Hell Night runs to earn coins. Spend them on snake skins and board skins in the Shop, open a private chat room (1,000 coins), or send coins to another player from your Account screen. The difficulty is fixed for the whole game. Your coins, skins and progress live on the game server, not in this browser.
